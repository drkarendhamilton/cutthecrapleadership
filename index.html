The Book

Four things stand between every leader and their results.

Crap is everything that sits between a leader and the results they are responsible for: the busywork nobody can justify, the meeting that should have been a sentence, the politics you pretend not to see.

CC

Complacency

Eats your results long before the decline shows up on a report.

RR

Red Tape

Teaches your best people that looking busy matters more than getting anything done.

AA

Assumptions

Cost you good decisions and good people at the same time.

PP

Politics

Rewards whoever manages the room and drives out whoever tells the truth.

Cut those four and what’s left is clarity. Every chapter gives you one real story, one thing to cut, and one thing to do this week.

About the Author

Dr. Karen Hamilton, DSL

Doctor of Global Strategic Leadership

She led her first team at fifteen, before she had the title. Since then she has turned failing programs around, served as Vice President of Academic Affairs and Dean of Business and Online Programs across many institutions, built a university from zero students, and led more than 100 doctoral students to the finish line.

Today she serves as founding Chancellor of Glory Christian University. She is an award-winning educator, the author of The Christian Millennial, and a mentor through nonprofit and workforce training programs. She has been underestimated, out-politicked, and buried in red tape, and has been the leader deciding whether to cut it. Long before this book, “cut the crap” was how she led.
